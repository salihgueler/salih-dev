A conference is often described through its schedule. The more lasting part is
the network of permissions it creates: permission to ask, contribute, disagree,
and return.

The strongest events I have attended paid attention to the spaces around the
talks. They gave first-time speakers a clear path in, made breaks long enough
for real conversation, and published useful material after the room emptied.

## Design for the second interaction

The first interaction is an introduction. The second is where a community
starts to become dependable.

Good event design helps that second interaction happen through recurring
meetups, public notes, small working groups, and introductions made with enough
context to be meaningful.
