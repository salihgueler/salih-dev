There is a difference between sharing what you are learning and turning every
hour of learning into a performance.

Public notes are most useful when they preserve the trail:

- what I expected;
- what the system actually did;
- which explanation survived contact with evidence;
- what I would test next.

This structure makes a note useful even when its conclusion changes. It also
gives readers something better than confidence: a way to inspect the reasoning.

## Prefer timestamps to certainty

“As of this week, this is how I understand it” is not timid language. It is a
version boundary. Software changes, teams learn, and advice expires.

The goal is not to be permanently right. The goal is to leave behind a compact,
honest artifact that another person can test.
