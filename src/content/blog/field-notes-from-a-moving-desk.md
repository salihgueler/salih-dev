Changing location can make every day feel like a fresh start. That is pleasant
for a weekend and expensive for a working month.

I now keep three things deliberately boring:

1. the first hour of the day;
2. the window when teammates can expect a reply;
3. the short ritual that marks work as finished.

The desk can move. The agreements should not have to.
